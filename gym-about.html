Built for the week you're actually training, not a program you have to follow exactly.

The Gym Planner started with the same question as the Run Analyzer: why does planning a workout week need a subscription and a rigid template you can't touch? It shouldn't.

What is the Gym Planner?

It's a free, no-login weekly workout planner. Describe your goal in a sentence and it generates a full week's split — or build one manually from scratch. Every exercise, every session, every day is yours to edit. Check things off as you go, log completed days to a history with real dates, and track your streaks, totals, and goals on a dashboard, all saved in your browser.

Why was it created?

Because generic templates never quite fit, and fully custom programs from an app usually sit behind a paywall. The Gym Planner gives you a real, sensible starting week for free, immediately, and then gets out of your way — rename a split, swap an exercise, add a second session on leg day, whatever you need.

The problem it's solving

Most workout planning tools force a choice: a rigid pre-built program you can't adjust, or a blank spreadsheet you have to fill in entirely yourself. The Gym Planner starts you off with a real plan based on what you actually want, then hands you full editing freedom — no locked templates, no subscription wall, no account required to keep using it.

What it stands for

No accounts, ever

Your plan and history are stored in this browser only — nothing is synced or sent anywhere.

Generated, never locked

Every auto-generated plan is a starting point. Nothing about it is fixed — rename it, restructure it, replace it.

Honest, general guidance

Exercise suggestions are general-purpose templates, not personalized medical or professional training advice.
