What is Lane One?

Lane One is a free, no-login running analyzer. Type in the distance and time of a run, and it hands back your pace, effort, calories, splits, and a written breakdown — plus a dashboard to track your history, personal bests, streaks, and goals over time, all saved right in your browser.

Why was Lane One created?

It started with a simple question: why does checking your pace need an app download, a login screen, and access to your location? It shouldn't. Lane One was built to prove that a genuinely useful running tool doesn't need any of that — just two numbers and a browser tab.

The problem it's solving

Most running apps are built to be a permanent training log first and an analysis tool second — which means a signup wall before you can see anything useful. Lane One flips that: you get the real analysis immediately, and only decide to save your history afterward, if you want to. No barrier between you and knowing how your run actually went.

What it stands for

No accounts, ever

Nothing is stored, nothing is synced. Refresh the page and your run is gone — that's intentional, not a missing feature.

Honest estimates

Calorie counts, effort zones, and comparisons are clearly labelled as estimates based on standard formulas — not personalized medical or training advice.

Analysis you can actually read

Instead of a wall of charts, Lane One writes your results back to you in plain language — like a coach glancing at your watch and telling you what it means.
