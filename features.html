Everything one run tells you.

Every one of these comes from a single distance-and-time entry. Nothing here needs a login, a watch, or a phone sensor.

Pace & speed breakdown

Your pace is calculated in both min/km and min/mile, alongside your average speed — so you're never stuck converting units in your head.

Pace-based intensity

An animated dial places your pace on an easy-to-sprint scale. It's based on pace alone — not heart rate or how the run actually felt — so treat it as a rough read, not a verdict.

Run summary

A short, written analysis — generated from your specific numbers — covering your distance category, effort zone, and a practical tip for your next session.

Calorie estimate

Uses standard MET (metabolic equivalent) formulas based on your speed, and your weight if you provide it, to estimate energy burned. Defaults to 70kg if left blank.

Nature comparisons

Your speed is measured against real reference points — a garden snail, a racing greyhound, a cheetah, Usain Bolt's world-record sprint — for a more intuitive sense of how fast you actually moved.

Session badges

Milestones like 5K finisher, 10K finisher, marathon distance, sub-5:00 pace, or an hour-plus effort are checked and unlocked live against your run.

Shareable result card

A clean, downloadable image summarizing your pace and stats — ready to post or send, generated entirely in your browser.

Unit switching

Enter and view your run in kilometers or miles — every figure converts automatically.

Day / night track

A light and dark theme, so the analyzer is comfortable to read whatever time you're logging your run.
