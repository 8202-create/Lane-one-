Why Lane One's Gym Planner exists

Most workout-planning apps either lock real programs behind a subscription, or hand you a rigid template you can't touch. This does the opposite: describe your goal in plain language, get a real weekly split back immediately, and then change anything about it — swap an exercise, add a second session on leg day, rename a split — without asking anyone's permission.

How it works
1

Describe your goal

"4 days a week, build muscle, upper/lower split" — or anything close to that. Lane One turns it into a full week.

2

Edit it until it's yours

Rename a day's split, add or remove exercises, or stack a second session on any day — nothing is locked.

3

Check off and log

Tick exercises off as you complete them, then log the day to your history with the date — visible on your dashboard.

What you get

Plan generator

Describe your goal and days available in plain language, and get a complete, sensible weekly split back instantly.

Two sessions per day

Morning cardio and evening lifting on the same day? Stack a second session on any day you want.

Full editing freedom

Every generated plan is a starting point — rename splits, swap exercises, or clear a day entirely.

Workout history & dashboard

Log completed sessions with the date, then track your streak, totals, and achievements over time.
