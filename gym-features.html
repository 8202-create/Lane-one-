Everything the Gym Planner does.

Built to get you a usable plan fast, then get out of your way while you edit it.

Plan generator

Describe your goal, days available, and preferred style in plain language, and get a complete weekly split back instantly — no template picking required.

Two sessions per day

Add a second session to any day — useful for a morning cardio session and an evening lift, or an upper/lower double day.

Full editing freedom

Rename any session's split, add or remove exercises, or edit an exercise's name entirely — nothing generated is locked in place.

Per-day checklist

Tick off exercises as you complete them, with progress visible right in the week overview at a glance.

Workout history with real dates

Log a completed day to your history with the actual calendar date — not just the weekday — so your dashboard reflects reality.

Dashboard & streaks

Total workouts, distinct days trained, exercises completed, current streak, and week-over-week comparison, all computed from your saved history.

Goals

Set targets like workouts per week, a monthly total, or a streak length, and track progress with a live bar.

Achievements

Milestones like your first logged workout, 10 or 50 workouts total, and 3/7/30-day streaks unlock automatically.

Weekly checkmark reset

Clear the week's checkboxes without losing your plan or your history — ready to go again next week.
