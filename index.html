Why Lane One exists

Most running tools want a signup, a subscription, and permission to track your location before they'll tell you anything about the run you just did. Lane One works the other way around: you tell it what happened, and it hands back a real analysis — pace, effort, calories, and context — with nothing saved and nothing asked for in return.

How it works
1

Enter your run

Distance, time, and — if you want a sharper calorie estimate — your weight. Nothing else required.

2

Get an instant breakdown

Pace, average speed, an effort gauge, a written coach's read, and a comparison against real-world reference speeds.

3

Share it or walk away

Download a share card if you want one. Nothing is stored — refresh the page and it's gone, by design.

What you get

Effort gauge

A visual read of exactly how hard that pace was, from easy to sprint effort.

Coach's read

A short written analysis of your distance, pace, and pacing strategy — like a coach glancing at your watch.

Nature comparisons

See your speed measured against everything from a garden snail to Usain Bolt's top speed.

Session badges

Milestones like 5K finisher or sub-5:00 pace, unlocked live based on the run you just entered.
